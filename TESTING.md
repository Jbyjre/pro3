# Check the 15-minute verse popup cooldown on the phone — 27 November

1. **Install the updated `pro.apk`** from `Phone → App updates → Check now` (or the latest GitHub release). Your chosen apps, colors and Psalm 51:10 setting stay untouched, and **Breathe → Cooldown** now defaults to **15 min**.
2. **First open pauses, then 15 minutes of quiet.** Open a chosen app: the `PSALM 51:10` layer appears. Close it to Home and reopen it, tap a notification for it, or open another chosen app inside the next 15 minutes: **no popup appears** (`Recent decisions` shows `"no pause: cooling down after a recent pause"`). After 15 minutes pass, the next chosen app you open shows the popup again — at most 4 times in an hour.

# Check the pause rule, colors, exits and updates on the phone — 19 November

Cloud tests cannot see a real Galaxy's Home screen, Recents, notifications, grayscale mode or installer. Do these on the S26 FE and write down what you see.

1. **Get this build first.** The updater on your phone is the old one, so open the newest "pro stable" release page in your browser (github.com/Jbyjre/pro3/releases/latest) and install `pro.apk` over pro, or use Phone → App updates → Check now. After that, opening pro checks by itself.
2. **The pause rule.** In Breathe choose Gmail and Claude. (a) Open Gmail, open Claude, then go back to Gmail by swiping or Recents: no pause. (b) Close Gmail from Recents, then open it from its icon a few seconds later: a pause. (c) Open Recents and return to Gmail at once: no pause. (d) Get a notification from Gmail and tap it: a pause. (e) Lock and unlock into Gmail: no pause (unless it was off a long time). After each, open Breathe → **Recent decisions** and read the reasons; tap Copy and send me the list if anything is wrong. It also lists the Home/Recents window names Samsung uses, which settle the one thing I could not verify.
3. **Per-app rules.** In Choose apps tap the gear on one app: set Every time and a 10 s length; it should pause every time and run 10 s while other apps follow the general rule.
4. **Verse and colors.** The layer should say only PSALM 51:10, steady, no other words. Try Your words. Choose a few colors and Random vs In order; confirm the text turns dark or light as the color passes over it.
5. **Exits.** Try Fade, Lift, Drop, Iris, Slide, Zoom and Instant, looking for any flash or hard cut.
6. **Grayscale.** Turn on Android's grayscale (Settings → Accessibility → Color adjustment, or Digital Wellbeing's grayscale). With Grayscale phone on Automatic, the layer should switch to Mono; if your phone's own grayscale is not noticed, choose Always and tell me which setting you used.
7. **Updates.** With Auto Blocker **on**, tap Update: expect the plain explanation and the Security settings button. Turn Auto Blocker off (Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker), update, turn it back on. Try Install another way, and Copy details. Overnight, if an update was downloaded, a "pro update ready" notification should appear instead of a stalled install.
8. **Release.** After the merge, the repository's Releases page should show a new "pro stable · build N" (not marked pre-release), with the change written in its notes.

# Check the smoother, once-per-visit Breathe on the phone — 18 November

Cloud tests cannot measure a real Galaxy's event delivery, screen compositing, gestures or haptics. These are the checks that can only be done on the S26 FE. Write down what you see; never assume it is fine because tests are green.

1. **Update first.** Open pro (it now checks for a new build by itself, at most every six hours). Phone → App updates should show "Last checked …". Update through the in-app button, then confirm Breathe still says "Ready when you open".
2. **Instant lay-over, cold and warm.** Swipe a chosen app away from Recents (cold start) and open it from its icon, then open it again after pressing Home (warm). Screen-record at 120 Hz. The first frame of the layer should be a solid dark shade of the chosen color, never a different color, and the app should never visibly restart. Then open Breathe → How Breathe works: **Last pause** shows how many milliseconds the layer took after Android reported the app and how long its first picture took. Record both numbers for cold and warm. Android reports an app after its window exists, so some exposure of the app's first frame is expected; note how long it is.
3. **No flicker, no cut-off.** During a pause: have someone message you (a heads-up notification), press volume up/down, pull the shade where possible. The pause must carry on to its end. Watch for any flash, double-draw, strip of the app above or below the color, or a hard cut at the end. Try the Fade, Lift and Instant transitions.
4. **Once per visit.** Open Gmail, then Claude, then Gmail again: only the first open of each pauses. Lock and unlock within the grace time: no pause. After the grace time (use 5 minutes to test faster) the next open pauses once. "Every open" pauses every time. Leaving to Home during a pause still counts as having paused.
5. **Chrome web apps.** With **Chrome chosen**, open the workout web app and the imposter game web app from their own icons: no pause. Open a normal Chrome tab: it pauses. Choose Chrome and not-chosen both. A plain "Add to Home screen" page shortcut opens an ordinary tab and is expected to pause. Confirm installed web apps no longer appear in Choose apps.
6. **Full screen and navigation.** With gesture navigation the color should reach the very top and bottom (no app strip). Repeat with three-button navigation: the layer should stop above the buttons and the buttons should still work. Rotate or fold during a pause: the layer should cover the new screen.
7. **Settings.** Length slider from 2 to 10 seconds: time a pause with a stopwatch from the layer appearing to the app fully visible. Change Words (Mix steps through all three sayings; picking one always shows it). Haptics should be a soft tick as the words change and one near the end; turn them off and confirm silence. Touch the screen during a pause: a soft light should follow your finger. Check Still color layer and Android's reduced-motion setting.
8. **Look and accessibility.** Large text (1.8×), light and dark theme, TalkBack on the slider and the choice rows, and a night-time glance at the three colors with their dark ink.
9. **Battery and background.** Over a day of normal use nothing in pro should show as a battery drain from Breathe. Restart the phone and confirm choices and "Ready when you open" survive.

# Check app-open breathing on the phone — 16 November

1. Install the new **correctly signed** build over pro; keep existing data. Open Breathe. Choose an ordinary app, tap Connect Breathe and enable **pro · Breathe** in Android Accessibility. If restricted, expand Connection help, use the App info link and Android's Allow restricted settings. Return: it must say **Ready when you open** only when Android has enabled and connected the service. No shortcut, Bluetooth, overlay permission, usage access or notification access is needed for breathing.
2. In Choose apps, tap the app name or anywhere on its row, then use Chosen to review the list. Try large text, long app names, search within Chosen and deselection. Repeat in landscape or split screen with the keyboard open: scroll to results, select an app and scroll back to search; controls must remain reachable. Use Done to return to Breathe; confirm the count updates and the keyboard closes. Try clear search, Reset search after no results, and selection haptics with Android touch feedback enabled/disabled. Open a newly chosen app immediately; removing a choice must release its pause without waiting for metadata reload.
3. Open a selected app with its **original icon**, then via a notification, a web/deep link from another app and Recents. Measure event/open-to-first-layer separately from the **2.5 seconds from the first breathing frame**. Record a screen video; the Android event can arrive after the destination's first frame, so cloud tests cannot certify zero exposure or subsecond Samsung latency. Logcat tag `Breathe` records attach-to-first-draw time without package names or screen content.
4. Confirm the exact conversation/article opened by a notification/link remains after the pause; no launch of the app's home activity. Try app-internal activity switches, dialogs, keyboards, rapid A→B→A and Home→same-app reentry. One layer only; duplicate events never extend it.
5. During the pause use Home, Back (button and gesture), lock, notification shade and incoming calls. No stuck touch target, delayed app launch or replay. Try rotation, split screen and display-size changes. Accessibility window ordering and gestures require real-device validation.
6. Try Cobalt/Tangerine/Pool, Still mode, system animation scales 0×/1×/10× and 1.8× text. Tap Replay: the preview should finish once, stop when pro backgrounds, and stay finished when returning. Toggle reduced motion while it plays; confirm it stops. Check light/dark themes, stacked large-text palette samples, expandable Connection help and How Breathe works. Preview and live layer should match; duration stays 2.5 seconds. Breathe has no off/pause/tile control. Old disabled/snoozed preferences must not bypass it. Removing an app from the chosen list removes coverage for that app.
7. Close pro's task, restart the phone, revoke/regrant access, force-stop and reopen pro, then upgrade. Confirm selections survive and connection status is accurate. Android can stop/revoke access; the app must never claim connected when it isn't. A force-stop is not something pro can bypass. If the page reports “Breathe needs attention,” open a chosen app again or use Check connection. Verify successful retry clears that state; it must not be confused with missing Accessibility access.
8. Recheck AirPods/Beats listening controls, music, heart readings and temporary pop-ups. Routine headphone events should not cover or extend the breath. Keep original app identity/signature and existing update flow. Follow the updater and headphone checks in [the previous checklist](docs/BREATHING_AND_UPDATES.md).

Cloud unit/integration/render tests are recorded in [the delivery notes](docs/APP_OPEN_BREATHING.md). They do not measure a physical Galaxy's event delivery, compositor timing, gesture behavior, background retention or Bluetooth.

## Retained headphone and updater checks

1. **Pop-ups and headphones.** Connect AirPods and Beats, play/pause/skip music, change listening mode, remove/reinsert a bud, and check low-battery behavior when possible. Useful controls should still work. Repeat connection broadcasts/reconnects must not keep extending a pop-up. It should dismiss on lock/rotation and leave no touch target. Routine connection/music events during breathing should be dropped, never lengthen it. System shade, calls and installer confirmation remain usable.
2. **Permissions.** In Phone, open each missing access control, grant it in Android Settings, and return: the prompt must disappear or the Update button must change from Allow pro updates to Update. Revoke each access and return: recovery controls must reappear. Test notification denial and its Notification settings link; if music access is restricted, use pro’s App info to allow restricted settings before retrying. Breathing must continue working without these accesses.
3. **Update once.** Phone > App updates > Check now. Confirm the real installed build number and chosen channel. Download a newer build and tap Update. Allow pro to install apps once if Android asks. A required system confirmation should open automatically for the foreground action; approve it. Settings and enabled features should survive, then open pro once if Android/OEM recovery did not resume headphones. Test denied install permission, cancelled confirmation, offline checks, and retry. Samsung Auto Blocker may require an explicit security choice; pro cannot bypass it. Never uninstall as the normal update path.
4. **Optional idle installation.** Enable it only if wanted, finish important recording sessions, then let the phone idle on Wi-Fi without music/calls. Observe whether Android silently updates or posts confirmation. Report which occurred; this has not been verified on a physical Samsung. Stable cannot downgrade a higher preview version. No repeated nagging should occur from cancelled/blocked updates.

For a freeze/crash, open Settings > Troubleshooting > Freezes and crashes and copy details. Never share the private signing backup in an issue or public message.
